If you're still relying exclusively on JPG or PNG on your web, you're most likely wasting bandwidth. It's not a matter of trends: modern formats **WebP** and **AVIF** were created for a very simple reason: to make the internet load faster.

Let's see what they are and why they're better than the classic ones.

## What is WebP?

It's a format that Google released a few years ago specifically with the web in mind. The idea was simple: to have a file that weighed much less than a JPG but looked just as good. And they succeeded.

### The best of WebP:

- It weighs around **30% less** than a JPEG with the same quality.
- It allows transparent backgrounds (goodbye to having to use heavy PNGs just for transparency).
- It's supported by practically all current browsers.

> **Summary**: It's the modern "safe" standard. If in doubt, use WebP.

## And what is AVIF?

AVIF is the next step. Based on the AV1 video codec, it's a newer and more aggressive technology (in a good way) with compression. It achieves ridiculously small files while maintaining surprising sharpness.

### The best of AVIF:

- It can reduce the weight by half compared to JPEG or even improve on WebP.
- It maintains details and colors very well (supports HDR) even when compressing heavily.
- Increasingly compatible: Chrome, Firefox, Safari and Edge already read it without problems.

> **Summary**: It's the format with the **best compression** that exists right now for general web use.

## Quick comparison table

| Feature              | JPEG     | PNG      | WebP     | AVIF     |
|----------------------|----------|----------|----------|----------|
| Compression          | Low      | Low      | Good     | Excellent|
| Transparency         | No       | Yes      | Yes      | Yes      |
| Animation            | No       | No       | Yes      | Yes      |
| Support              | Universal| Universal| Total    | Very High|
| File size            | High     | Very High| Medium   | Very Low |

## So, which one should you use?

- **WebP**: If you want to play it safe and guarantee that 100% of your users (even with very old browsers) see the image.
- **AVIF**: If you're looking for maximum performance and speed. Nowadays it's the best technical option.

Many professional sites use a mixed strategy: they try to load AVIF first, and if the browser is old, they load WebP.
