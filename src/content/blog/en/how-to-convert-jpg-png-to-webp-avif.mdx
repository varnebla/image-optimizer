## What are WebP and AVIF and why are they important?

WebP and AVIF are modern image standards designed specifically for the web. Their main objective is to offer the **maximum visual quality with the smallest possible file size**.

| Format | Main Advantage |
|--------|-------------------|
| WebP   | Excellent compatibility and balance between quality and weight. |
| AVIF   | Superior compression, ideal for those seeking maximum optimization. |

> **Important fact**: The AVIF format is capable of reducing the size of an image by up to 50% more than a traditional JPEG without the human eye perceiving any loss of quality.
