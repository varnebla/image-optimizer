On many websites, images account for more than 50% of the total page weight. This has a direct impact on loading speed, user experience and SEO positioning. Therefore, optimizing the images of a web project is absolutely essential.

## What is image optimization?

Optimizing images consists of reducing the size of graphic files, like JPEG, PNG, WebP or AVIF, without losing their visual quality. This is achieved mainly through:

- **Compression**: reducing the amount of data in the image.
- **Resizing**: adjusting the real dimensions to what is just necessary, avoiding excessive sizes.
- **Change of format**: choosing a more modern and efficient format like WebP or AVIF.

## How do heavy images affect your web?

Large and uncompressed images can generate several serious problems:

- **Slow loading speed**: every extra second increases the probability of abandonment.
- **High bounce rate**: users don't wait if the content doesn't load fast.
- **SEO penalty**: Google values loading speed when establishing ranking. Heavy images negatively affect Core Web Vitals.

> If your site takes more than 3 seconds to load, you could lose up to 53% of visitors, according to [data from Hostinger](https://www.hostinger.com/ca/tutorials/website-load-time-statistics).

## Benefits of optimizing your images

- Significant improvement in PageSpeed Insights score.
- Faster loading, especially on mobile and slow connections.
- Higher user retention and better conversion rates.
- Better positions in search engines thanks to a better user experience.
